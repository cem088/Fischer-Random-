Chess960 Rules

Setup: pawns start as usual. The back rank is shuffled so that the bishops sit on opposite colors and the king stands somewhere between the two rooks. Black mirrors White. That gives 960 possible positions.

Castling: works like standard chess in its end result. Queenside (O-O-O): king goes to the c-file and rook to the d-file. Kingside (O-O): king goes to the g-file and rook to the f-file, wherever they started — even if one doesn’t move at all.

Every square between the king and rook and their destinations must be empty (apart from those two pieces). The king can’t be in check, nor cross or land on an attacked square. Neither piece may have moved before.

In this game: select your king, then click the rook you want to castle with (shown with a ring). Pawns promote to a queen automatically.
